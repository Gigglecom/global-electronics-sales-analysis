Interactive Microsoft Excel sales dashboard analyzing revenue, profit, products, customers, countries, and sales channels using Power Query, Power Pivot, DAX, PivotTables, and PivotCharts.
# Global Electronics Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the sales performance of a global electronics retailer using Microsoft Excel.

The objective was to transform raw transactional data into useful business insights and develop an interactive dashboard that allows users to explore sales performance across products, countries, years, and sales channels.

The project contains more than 62,000 sales records covering the period from 2016 to 2021.

## Business Questions

The analysis focused on answering questions such as:

- What is the company's total revenue and profit?
- How have sales changed over time?
- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- Which products perform best?
- Which countries generate the most sales?
- How do online sales compare with physical-store sales?
- How many customers and orders does the company have?

## Data Model

The project uses a relational data model consisting of:

- Sales
- Customers
- Products
- Stores
- Exchange Rates

The Sales table serves as the main fact table and is connected to the other tables using customer, product, store, and exchange-rate keys.

## Key Insights

### 1. Revenue Performance

The company generated approximately $55.76 million in total revenue and $32.66 million in profit.

### 2. Product Performance

Computers were the highest-performing product category, generating approximately $19.30 million and contributing about 34.6% of total revenue.

### 3. Sales Channel Performance

Physical stores generated approximately $44.35 million, representing around 79.5% of total revenue.

Online sales generated approximately $11.40 million.

### 4. Geographic Performance

The United States was the largest physical-store market, generating approximately $23.76 million in revenue.

### 5. Sales Trend

2019 recorded the highest annual sales performance in the dataset.

The 2021 figures represent only a partial year and should therefore not be directly compared with complete years.
